FedEx Freight's share of public LTL shipments fell from 43% to 36%

Share of average daily shipments among FedEx Freight, Old Dominion, Saia, XPO and ArcBest. Source: SEC filings.

Among the five largest public LTL carriers, FedEx Freight's share of daily shipments fell from 43 to 36 percent between 2019 and mid-2026.
